Overseas expense control: turning a payment process nobody could describe into a system that shipped 海外费控系统：把一条说不清的付款流程，做成能上线的系统

Second product manager · May–Sep 2026 · phase-one requirements and product solution 二号产品经理 · 2026.05–09 · 一期需求与产品方案

2026.09.01 System went live 系统上线
20 days early提前 20 天 Development finished ahead of plan 开发完成早于计划
3 approval flows3 条审批流 Contract · payment · write-off 合同 · 付款单 · 核销单
30-second summary30 秒摘要

What was wrong, what I decided, what changed 问题在哪、我判断了什么、最后变了什么

Problem问题

Contracts, invoices and payments had no shared reference, so nobody could answer "has this been paid, and how much?" without digging by hand. 合同、发票、付款之间没有单号关联，「这笔付了吗、付了多少」只能靠人工翻记录回答。

Three judgements三个判断
  • A bookkeeping step does not belong in the approval chain.记账动作不该占用审批链的签字。
  • Phase one is scoped by what can ship, not by what is complete.一期范围用「能不能上线」定，不用「完不完整」定。
  • Clean the master data before go-live, not after.主数据要先清洗再上线，不能上线后再补。
Result结果

Live on 2026.09.01, development 20 days early; 85%+ of invoice and contract records, 90%+ of supplier records and 70%+ of in-transit documents loaded into the master-data hub; dual-flow match rate above 80%; 1–2 months of manual finance work removed. 2026.09.01 上线、开发提前 20 天；85%+ 发票合同、90%+ 供应商、70%+ 在途单据进入主数据中心；双流程勾稽率 80%+；减少财务 1–2 个月人工。

Background背景与约束

Why this was hard — and it was not the "business process re-engineering" kind of hard 为什么这件事难——不是流程再造那种难

Six pain points came out of the kick-off and the finance interviews. Every one of them is a question somebody could not answer. 启动会和财务访谈里暴露出六个痛点，每一个都是「有人答不上来的一个问题」。

1. No thread between documents1. 单据之间没有关联

Contracts, invoices and payments had no historical reference, so auditing and payment lookups were slow.合同、发票与付款环节缺乏历史单号关联，审核及付款记录查询效率低。

2. Payment progress was invisible2. 付款进度看不见

Whether a contract was paid, how much was paid and how much was left had no visual tracking — business users asked finance by chat.合同是否完成付款、已付与未付金额没有可视化追踪，业务只能反复问财务。

3. Entry errors carried money risk3. 录入错误带着资金风险

Payment details were typed in by hand with little validation, leaving room for wrong or late payments to suppliers.付款信息录入缺少有效校验，存在向供应商错付、迟付的资金风险。

4. Re-entry created new errors4. 重复录入制造新错误

Submitters re-typed the same information, so they used "submit again" to save effort — which is exactly where mistakes crept in.提单需要录入大量重复信息，大家频繁用「再次提交」简化操作，反而更容易错漏。

5. Signing sat outside the process5. 签署游离在流程外

Contract signing links were emailed outside the approval system, so the signed version could differ from the approved one.签署链接通过邮箱独立发送、未集成进审批系统，存在版本不一致与被篡改的风险。

6. Approval ran late for month-end close6. 审批赶不上月结

Approvals could land after the expense month had closed, and the document approval flow was tightly coupled to the payment flow — which tied accrual-basis month-end close to cash-basis payment in one chain.审批可能晚于费用月结，而单据审批流与付款流耦合严重——等于把月结的权责发生制和付款的收付实现制绑在同一条链上。

Constraints约束条件
Invoice formats票据形态 Overseas invoices come in every shape, so documents could not be standardised on entry. 海外票据形式难以统一，无法在入口处做成标准格式。
System boundaries系统边界 Four external systems to agree boundaries with: master-data hub, SAP, Feishu, Docusign. 四个外部系统要谈清边界：主数据中心、SAP、飞书、Docusign。
Deadline交付时间 Phase one still had to go live, on the date already committed to. 一期仍然必须在既定日期上线。

Gathering information我怎么拿到信息

Ask the right people, then cross-check what they say 先问对的人，再交叉验证

Finance财务

Owns the accounting rules and the compliance risk: what must be traceable, what counts as paid.掌握核算口径与合规风险：什么必须可追溯、什么才算已付。

Business users业务

Describe the real path, including the shortcuts they invented — re-submitting old forms instead of retyping.讲真实操作路径，包括他们自己发明的捷径：直接「再次提交」旧单据。

Engineering研发

Tell me what the external systems can actually return, which decides where the boundary has to sit.告诉我外部系统实际能返回什么，这决定了边界画在哪里。

Example. On "payment progress" the two sides were describing different things: business users wanted "was this month's payment made, and how much", finance wanted the books to reconcile. The product answer was one place that answers the question instead of a person answering it. 举个例子。「付款进度」这件事，业务说的和财务说的不是一回事：业务要的是「这个月付了吗、付了多少」，财务要的是账实一致。产品上的答案是把答案放在一个地方，而不是让某个人反复去答。
Judgement 1 of 3判断一 / 三

Decouple the bookkeeping step from the approval chain 把记账动作从审批链解耦出去

Source来源
The old flow tied approval and payment into one chain: approvals could land after the expense month closed, so accrual-basis close and cash-basis payment were coupled. On top of that sat a node where a person adjusted the expense breakdown and decided whether to push the voucher to SAP.旧流程把审批和付款绑在同一条链上：审批可能晚于费用月结，月结的权责发生制与付款的收付实现制因此耦合在一起。链上还有一个节点，由人工调整费用明细，并判断是否把凭证推送到 SAP。
Conflict冲突
Keeping it in the approval chain meant every approver signed off on a bookkeeping action. Removing it outright made finance nervous about compliance.留在审批链里，等于每个审批人都要替一个记账动作签字；直接删掉，财务担心合规。
Judgement判断
Separate the accounting from the approval chain. The requirement document already said it: this information only serves the accounting ledger, does not affect the business-reasonableness check, and is an accessory to the main approval item. So the step left the chain — after the first finance review the system opens a pending item in the voucher module, and the accountant's confirmation writes back to the document.把核算从审批链里拆出去，模块分离。需求文档里其实已经写清楚了：这部分信息只面向财务核算账目、不影响前端业务合理性审核，属于主审批事项的附属信息。所以财务初审后系统自动生成核算凭证的待处理项，核算人员确认后回写单据。
Result结果
The node left the chain: a shorter approval path, accounting separated from approval, accrual reflected more fully, and a shorter month-end close for the expense module. And because the written-back entry does not overwrite the original expense breakdown, the gap between what the submitter understood and what finance booked stays on record — data for improving the process later.这个节点离开了审批链：审批链更短，记账与审批分离，权责发生体现得更充分，费控模块的月结周期也随之缩短；同时回写不覆盖原费用明细，于是「提单人的理解」和「财务的入账」之间的差异沉下来，变成后续优化流程的数据。
Judgement 2 of 3判断二 / 三

Phase one is scoped by what can ship, not by what is complete 一期范围用「能不能上线」定，不用「完不完整」定

Source来源
Online payment (bank integration) and AI-based risk alerts both sat on the wish list, and both are genuinely valuable.线上付款（银企直联）和 AI 预警都在愿望清单上，而且都确实有价值。
Conflict冲突
Doing everything meant shipping nothing on time; cutting them meant explaining why.全做等于一期交不出来；砍掉又要解释为什么。
Judgement判断
Both were explicitly put out of scope, with the reason written next to them: overseas banking infrastructure cannot support direct bank integration yet, and alerts need samples that only exist once the main flow runs.两个都明确划出本期范围，并把原因写在旁边：海外银行基建暂时做不到银企直联；AI 预警需要样本，而样本只有主流程跑起来才有。
Result结果
Phase one concentrated on nine modules and three approval flows. The system went live on 2026.09.01 with development finishing 20 days early.一期集中在九个模块和三条审批流上，系统 2026.09.01 上线，开发提前 20 天完成。

Judgement 3 of 3判断三 / 三

Clean the master data before go-live — building the mining and cleanup capability that neither side could deliver on its own 先清洗主数据再上线——两边各自缺一块的能力，由我补上做成

Source来源
Supplier, contract and payment history had to be initialised. Without that, go-live meant opening a system on top of tens of thousands of documents nobody had tidied up.供应商、合同、付款的历史数据需要初始化；不做这一步，上线就是在几万份没整理过的旧单据上开系统。
Conflict冲突
Nobody on either side could do the whole job: finance had no data-mining or cleanup capability, and engineering had no way to judge financial boundaries. The composite task had no owner.两边都做不了这件事：财务没有数据挖掘与清洗能力，研发没有财务专业边界判断——这个复合任务没人现成会做。
Judgement判断
Neither side could do it, so the work was taken in-house along four steps — mining, cleaning, reconciliation, output — turning tens of thousands of legacy documents into data the master-data hub could accept. One design decision sits underneath all four: rules could not be enumerated, so semantic judgement plus scripts were split into 11 layered parallel sections. The figure below has the detail.既然两边都没有这条能力，这件事就自己做：按挖掘、清洗、勾稽、输出四步走，把上万份旧单据变成能进主数据中心的数据。四步底下压着一个设计决策——规则穷举不过来，就用语义判断结合脚本，拆成 11 个分层并行小节。细节见下面那张图。
Result结果
85%+ of invoice and contract records, 90%+ of supplier records and 70%+ of in-transit contracts and write-off records loaded into the master-data hub; dual-flow match rate above 80%; 1–2 months of manual finance work removed.85%+ 发票合同、90%+ 供应商、70%+ 在途合同与提款单核销记录进入主数据中心；双流程勾稽率 80%+；减少财务 1–2 个月人工。

If I ran it again如果重来一次

  • Settle the "details adjustment" ownership with finance in the first week — the approval chain could have been signed off in one pass instead of several rounds.第一周就和财务把「费用明细调整属于谁」定下来——审批流可以一次定稿，而不是来回改几轮。
  • Pull the master-data cleanup earlier. Initialisation ran alongside everything else, and it was the part with the least slack.主数据清洗应该更早介入。初始化是和其他事并行的，但它恰恰是最没有余量的一环。
  • Write the boundary questions down before the first design review, not during it.边界问题应该在第一次方案评审前就写下来，而不是在评审现场才问。

What this project taught me这件事教会我什么

  • "Who signs this?" is usually a better question than "which module does this belong to?"「这件事该谁签字」往往比「这件事归哪个模块」更有用。
  • A scope document is only real when it says what is not being built, and why.范围文档真正的价值在于写清楚「不做什么」以及为什么。
  • Data cleanup is not preparation for launch; it is part of the product, because without it the system has nothing to show.数据清洗不是上线的准备工作，它就是产品的一部分——没有它，系统上去也没东西可看。
