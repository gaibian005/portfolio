Document master-data extraction & cleanup 单据主数据清洗与重构

Data owner · 2026 · Tens of thousands of files, two disconnected workflows 数据负责人 · 2026 · 上万份文件 · 两条互不相连的流程
  • Split the cleanup into 11 layered parallel sections to cut database write contention and model drift. 把清洗拆成 11 个分层并行小节，降低数据库写入冲突与模型注意力发散。
  • Text-layer extraction first, OCR fallback for scans, multimodal parsing for hard layouts — all mapped into SQLite as JSON. 文字层提取优先、扫描件 OCR 兜底、复杂排版用多模态，统一输出 JSON 映射进数据库。
  • Matched records through two independent trails: file fingerprint (MD5, size, text) versus the document-to-SAP path. 两套独立线索勾稽：文件指纹（哈希、大小、文本）对比单据到 SAP 的链路。
  • Low-confidence and conflicting records go to a separate human queue with a recorded reason — never a silent failure. 失败与低置信记录进入独立待处理队列并记录原因，绝不静默失败。

EU product-access compliance study 欧盟产品准入合规研究

KPMG briefing on EU market access · research and company-view analysis 毕马威欧盟产品准入会议纪要 · 研究与公司视角分析
  • Worked through three regimes — IAA, CBAM and the Deforestation Regulation — and their timelines. 梳理三部主要法案及其时间节点：工业加速法案 IAA、碳边境调节机制 CBAM、零毁林监管法案 DR。

How I work工作方式

Product work and AI work, in the same loop 产品与 AI，在同一条链路里

Product defines the problem and the acceptance bar; AI and scripts remove the repetitive part in between. 产品负责把问题和验收标准定义清楚，AI 与脚本负责把中间重复的部分做掉。

Product: six steps, each with an artefact产品：六步闭环，每步都有交付物

Every step produces something the next person can use — no step ends in a conversation only. 每一步都留下别人能接着用的东西，不把工作停在口头沟通上。
