How I work我怎么工作

Ask the right people, cross-check, then decide 问对的人，交叉验证，再变成判断

Complex problems are usually not solved by a clever idea. They are solved by getting the information straight first. 复杂的问题很少靠一个聪明点子解决，通常是先把信息对齐，剩下的选择自然就清楚了。

1 · Ask the right people1 · 问对的人

Finance, business users and developers each hold part of the answer. I talked to all three before writing anything. 财务、业务、研发各掌握一部分答案。写方案之前，这三方我都问了一遍。

Finance财务 Business业务 Engineering研发

2 · Cross-check what they say2 · 交叉验证

They did not always agree. I matched each claim against the approval records, kept the confirmed facts, and listed the rest as open items. 三方说法并不一致。我把每种说法和审批记录对了一遍，确认过的写进需求，没对齐的记成待确认清单。

Interviews访谈 Records审批记录 Open items待确认清单

3 · Turn it into a decision3 · 变成判断

Once the information lines up, the remaining choice is a product decision — and it has to be something a team can build. 信息对齐之后，剩下的选择就是产品判断——而且这个判断必须能落成研发做得了的东西。

Overseas expense control system海外费用控制系统

COROS Sports Technology · May–Sep 2026 · Second product manager 高驰 COROS · 财务部 · 2026.05–09 · 二号产品经理
  • Wrote the phase-one requirements and product solution: nine modules covering suppliers, contracts, invoices, documents (payment, reimbursement, write-off), accounting vouchers, master data, permissions, coding rules and statistics. 负责一期需求与产品方案：九个模块，覆盖供应商、合同、发票、单据（付款/报销/核销）、核算凭证、财务主数据、权限、编码规则与数据统计。
  • Led the cleanup of legacy documents into master data the system could go live on. 主导旧流程主数据清洗与重构，把上万份单据变成可上线使用的主数据。
  • From information to a decision: finance said the expense breakdown had to be adjusted inside the payment form, while business users never touched it — so I treated it as a bookkeeping step rather than an approval step and moved it out of the approval chain. 来源 → 判断：财务说费用明细要在付款单里调整，业务却从不看这一块 → 判断这是记账动作而不是审批动作，把它从审批链里拆出去。

Document master-data extraction & cleanup 单据主数据清洗与重构

Data owner · 2026 · Tens of thousands of files 数据负责人 · 2026 · 上万份文件
  • Mining: text-layer extraction, OCR and multimodal recognition on 10,000+ legacy documents; scripts plus LLM judgement to clean and restructure. 挖掘：文本层提取、OCR 与多模态识别，抓取上万份旧单据的关键数据；再用脚本加大模型语义判断清洗重构。
  • Reconciliation and output: MD5 hash, file size, text content and multi-hop SAP links pushed the dual-flow match rate above 80%; output was JSON and Excel fit for the master-data hub and finance audit. Failures went to a human queue with the reason recorded. 勾稽与输出：文件哈希 MD5、文件大小、文本内容与 SAP 多跳把双流程勾稽率做到 80%+，产出可进主数据中心与财务审计的 JSON 和 Excel；失败与低置信记录进人工队列并记录原因。

Market insight assistant · product design without a reference 行情翻译官与异动侦探 · 没有标准答案时怎么定义产品

Tencent AI Bootcamp · Product manager · Aug–Sep 2026 腾讯 AI 训练营 · 产品经理 · 2026.08–09
  • The problem: people see a watchlist stock move but not why. Market apps give numbers, not explanations, and never reach out first. 解决的问题：用户看到自选股异动却不知道发生了什么——行情产品只给数字不给解释，也不会主动找人。
  • There is no single right answer for why a price moved, so "verified / weak / unverifiable" all had to be legitimate outputs, and the launch bar had to be defined by me. 为什么没有标准答案：涨跌归因本就没有唯一正解，所以「有据 / 弱据 / 存疑」都要能作为正式输出，上线门槛也只能自己定。
  • The solution: a conversational assistant that pushes an alert the moment an anomaly fires, and attaches an expandable evidence chain to every conclusion instead of bluffing. 解决方案：做成对话式异动解读助手——异动发生时主动推送提醒，每条结论附可展开的证据链，弱据与存疑如实标注，而不是硬答。
Nine-step pipeline九步链路 Collect → normalise → rule alerts → RAG evidence → attribution 数据采集 → 标准化 → 规则监测 → RAG 取证 → 大模型归因 → fact / compliance check → conversation → human review → feedback loop → 事实合规校验 → 对话触达 → 人工分级审核 → 行为回流
