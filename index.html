How I work我怎么工作

Ask the right people, cross-check, then decide 问对的人，交叉验证，再变成判断

Complex problems are usually not solved by a clever idea. They are solved by getting the information straight first. 复杂的问题很少靠一个聪明点子解决，通常是先把信息对齐，剩下的选择自然就清楚了。

1 · Ask the right people1 · 问对的人

Finance, business users and developers each hold part of the answer. I talked to all three before writing anything. 财务、业务、研发各掌握一部分答案。写方案之前，这三方我都问了一遍。

Finance财务 Business业务 Engineering研发

2 · Cross-check what they say2 · 交叉验证

They did not always agree. I matched each claim against the approval records, kept the confirmed facts, and listed the rest as open items. 三方说法并不一致。我把每种说法和审批记录对了一遍，确认过的写进需求，没对齐的记成待确认清单。

Interviews访谈 Records审批记录 Open items待确认清单

3 · Turn it into a decision3 · 变成判断

Once the information lines up, the remaining choice is a product decision — and it has to be something a team can build. 信息对齐之后，剩下的选择就是产品判断——而且这个判断必须能落成研发做得了的东西。

Overseas expense control system海外费用控制系统

COROS Sports Technology · May–Sep 2026 · Second product manager 高驰 COROS · 财务部 · 2026.05–09 · 二号产品经理
  • Wrote the phase-one requirements and product solution: nine modules covering suppliers, contracts, invoices, payment write-offs, accounting vouchers, permissions and coding rules. 负责一期需求与产品方案：九个模块，含供应商、合同、发票、付款核销、核算凭证、权限与编码规则。

  • From information to a decision: finance said the expense breakdown had to be adjusted inside the payment form, while business users never touched it — so I treated it as a bookkeeping step rather than an approval step and moved it out of the approval chain. 来源 → 判断：财务说费用明细要在付款单里调整，业务却从不看这一块 → 判断这是记账动作而不是审批动作，把它从审批链里拆出去。
