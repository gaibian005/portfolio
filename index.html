◆ Supporting case

Document master-data extraction & cleanup

Data owner · 2026 · Tens of thousands of files, two disconnected workflows
  • Split the cleanup into 11 layered parallel sections to cut database write contention and model drift.
  • Text-layer extraction first, OCR fallback for scans, multimodal parsing for hard layouts — all mapped into SQLite as JSON.
  • Matched records through two independent trails: file fingerprint (MD5, size, text) versus the document-to-SAP path.
  • Low-confidence and conflicting records go to a separate human queue with a recorded reason — never a silent failure.
